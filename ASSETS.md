# Website image and favicon

The website image is an AI-generated conceptual editorial illustration, not documentation of an actual fire, location or smoke event. Generated using the built-in image-generation tool. The WebP version retains the complete composition and original dimensions; it is compressed for website delivery. The original PNG is included for reuse.

## Final image prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide editorial landscape illustration for an Australian public-evidence website named What's the Hazard? Primary request: a refined, quiet Australian eucalyptus landscape illustrating the relationship between bushland, communities and the atmosphere, suitable for a credible report website. Scene: rolling NSW bushland with recognisable eucalyptus trees in the foreground, a small cluster of suburban houses sitting at the forest edge in the middle distance, receding forested hills and an expansive pale sky. An extremely subtle translucent ribbon of atmospheric haze crosses the valley, ambiguous and gentle, not a dramatic smoke plume. Style: sophisticated contemporary editorial print illustration, elegant layered shapes, fine paper texture, carefully drawn gum leaves and pale eucalyptus trunks, subtle organic detail; beautifully composed, calm and sober. Palette coordinated with the website: deep eucalyptus teal #193c3f, sage green #cdd7cf, cream #faf9f4, restrained warm terracotta #9e441c used sparingly in roofs and earth. Wide landscape composition about 2:1, with the lower half carrying trees and settlement and upper half offering uncluttered sky. This is a conceptual illustration, not a photograph or evidence of a real fire. Constraints: no text, no typography, no logo, no question mark in the scene, no watermark, no flames, no black smoke, no distressed people, no firefighters, no Aboriginal people or Aboriginal art motifs, no warning triangles, no charts or statistics, no apocalyptic or propagandistic appearance. Polished art direction for a readable evidence-first website, soft daylight, no photorealism.

## Favicon design

A cream question-mark path on a deep eucalyptus-green rounded square, with a muted gold dot. It was drawn as editable SVG to remain legible at small sizes and coordinated with the site name. The SVG source is `favicon.svg` (also `favicon-question.svg`). PNG and ICO exports were rendered from this same SVG. The earlier WH icon is retained as `assets/favicon-wh.svg`.
